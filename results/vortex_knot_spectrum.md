# Vortex-knot mass spectrum (claim 17)

The three charged leptons are assigned to the torus knots T(2,3), T(2,5), T(2,7); the mass is proportional to the regularized thin-filament Biot-Savart energy at core radius 0.1.

## Test

- Koide Q of the knot energies: 0.339340 (target 2/3 = 0.666667).
- Koide Q of the knot lengths: 0.339843.
- Koide Q of the observed masses: 0.666664.
- Maximum relative ratio error: 0.999.
- Power p required by m ~ E^p: 12.279.
- **Status: FALSIFIED (thin-filament spectrum too compressed).**

A thin filament has E ~ L log(L/a); its ratios are O(1). Matching the charged-lepton hierarchy would need m ~ E^p with p about 12.3, or an exponentially small core. This test falsifies only the simple assignment, not the general vortex idea.
